Privacy policy

gog personal is a private tool used only by its owner, on their own computer, with their own Google account.

  • Data from Google APIs is read and changed only at the owner's request, on the owner's computer.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on that computer.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
